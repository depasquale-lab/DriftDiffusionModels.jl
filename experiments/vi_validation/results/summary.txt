### Stage: acf — is the §2.10 autocorrelation result a property of
###              the data, or an artefact of the inference procedure?

  Three conditions, identical pipeline:
    iid       trial parameters i.i.d.  → recovered ACF must be flat
    switch    sticky Markov chain      → recovered ACF must be positive
    shuffled  switch data, order permuted before fitting
                                       → recovered ACF must collapse

  seed=1 iid       mean lag-1 ACF: recovered -0.012   true -0.025
  seed=1 switch    mean lag-1 ACF: recovered +0.100   true +0.935
  seed=1 shuffled  mean lag-1 ACF: recovered +0.008   true -0.052
  seed=2 iid       mean lag-1 ACF: recovered -0.009   true -0.014
  seed=2 switch    mean lag-1 ACF: recovered +0.093   true +0.936
  seed=2 shuffled  mean lag-1 ACF: recovered -0.028   true +0.011

  → acf.csv, acf_summary.csv
  Read: 'iid' is the false-positive rate of the manuscript's diagnostic.
        'shuffled' is the artefact control. Both must be near zero for
        the §2.10 claim to hold.
